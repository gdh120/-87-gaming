Market 87

A planned marketplace for eligible gaming-related digital products and services.

Proposed commerce model

One-time purchases, managed in one place.

We are exploring a unified payment integration for different types of digital purchases across 87 Gaming. All purchases described here are planned, not currently available.

Community membership access would be time-limited and managed by 87 Gaming. A member would choose to make a new purchase when access expires — with no automatic renewal.
01

Digital creator gifts

Members purchase optional digital gifts associated with creators' content. Eligible creators may receive a share of the purchase value.

02

Community access passes

Members make a one-time purchase for a defined period of access to an independently managed community.

03

Marketplace purchases

Members buy eligible digital products or services from third-party sellers, subject to platform rules and payment-provider approval.

04

Platform commission & payouts

We aim to use an approved multiparty payments solution to deduct processing fees, allocate a disclosed 87 Gaming commission, and settle remaining funds to eligible recipients.

Payment integration status

87 Gaming is evaluating PayPal's platform payment capabilities, including checkout, recipient onboarding, platform fees, and settlement. Availability of these capabilities depends on PayPal's review, approval, and applicable requirements. No PayPal payment flow is active on this website.
